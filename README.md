## 💡 KEY THEMES TO WEAVE THROUGHOUT

Whenever possible, emphasize these in your answers:

- **Scale**: "We handled 100M+ records daily..." (numbers matter)
- **Production**: "In production, we had to handle..." (show battle scars)
- **Impact**: "This reduced costs by $500k/year..." (business language)
- **Collaboration**: "Worked across data science, platform, and ops teams..."
- **Learning**: "I'm constantly learning new frameworks..." (growth mindset)
- **Ownership**: "I owned the project end-to-end..." (no victim language)
- **Efficiency**: "Optimized the pipeline, cut latency by 60%..." (value-add)

## 📝 TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### Before the Interview
- [ ] Review your 5 best stories one last time
- [ ] Practice 3 tough questions aloud
- [ ] Get 8 hours of sleep the night before
- [ ] Arrive 15 minutes early
- [ ] Test your camera/audio if remote

### During the Interview
- [ ] Listen fully before answering
- [ ] Pause to think (2 seconds is fine)
- [ ] Use specific numbers and examples
- [ ] Tell stories, not bullet points
- [ ] Ask clarifying questions if confused
- [ ] Show enthusiasm for their challenges

### Red Flags to Avoid
- ❌ Vague answers ("I've done pipelines")
- ❌ Blaming others for failures
- ❌ Speaking negatively about past employers
- ❌ Pretending to know tools you don't
- ❌ Asking only about salary/perks
- ❌ Appearing disengaged or not curious

## 🆘 IF YOU GET STUCK

**Stuck on a question?**
- Re-read `02_20_INTERVIEW_QUESTIONS.md` for similar questions
- Use frameworks like STAR for behavioral questions
- Write down your thoughts before answering

**Feeling unprepared on a topic?**
- Jump to `04_RED_FLAGS_GAPS.md` for your specific gap
- Watch 1-2 YouTube videos on that topic (30-60 min)
- Build a small project to gain hands-on experience

**Nervous or anxious?**
- Remember: They want you to succeed
- You have 5+ years of experience (you belong here)
- Admitting gaps > pretending to know everything
- Breathe: 4 count in, hold 4 count, 6 count out (repeat 3x)
